Potential research question: “How does explicit per-file privacy feedback affect a user's confidence when organizing cloud files?” Keep this question exploratory unless you run a controlled study. A basic prototype can demonstrate authentication and rules, but it cannot establish that files are encrypted end-to-end or that users are protected against every threat.
